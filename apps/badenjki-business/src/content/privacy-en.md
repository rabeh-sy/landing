_Effective date_ 24 June 2026

We respect your privacy in Badenjki Business. This policy explains what data the app may store or process, how we use it, and when we may share it.

## 1. Data you enter

The app stores the business data you enter, such as customers, suppliers, products, invoices, purchases, payments, inventory, reports, settings, and business details. This data is stored primarily on your device so you can use the app without an internet connection.

## 2. Activation and your account

The app may use identifiers such as your installation ID, basic device information, license status, and account or business IDs to activate the app, check your subscription, connect devices, and provide support.

## 3. Optional sync

If you enable sync or connect a business, the app may send business records and settings to our servers to synchronize them across your devices or among authorized users in the same business. If you leave sync disabled, most business data stays on your device.

## 4. AI features

If you use AI features, the app may process messages, attachments, or relevant business data to help you search, analyze, import, create documents, or answer questions. The app applies changes proposed by AI only after you review and approve them.

## 5. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions when needed.

- Contacts to import customers or suppliers you select.
- Camera to scan barcodes.
- Microphone to record notes or voice commands for AI features.
- Notifications to show alerts such as low-stock warnings.

These permissions are optional. The app uses each permission for its associated feature.

## 6. Files, backups, and exports

You may be able to import files such as CSV or XLSX files, or export reports and backups. You control the files you choose or create. When you share a file or backup outside the app, you are responsible for choosing who receives it.

## 7. Diagnostics and service improvements

The app may collect diagnostics such as app version, operating system, errors, sync status, and a limited number of usage events. We use them to fix problems, improve reliability, provide support, and protect the service. We may use trusted external services to monitor crashes and analyze errors.

## 8. Data sharing

We do not sell your data or your business data. We share data only when needed to run the service, including hosting, activation, sync, AI, error monitoring, support, legal compliance, and app distribution.

## 9. Data retention and deletion

Local business data stays on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or replace the database from a backup. We may retain data on our servers, such as activation, sync, AI, and support data, as needed for service operations, security, support, and legal obligations. You can [delete your account](/en/account-deletion) with your phone number and password, or from Settings in the app. You can also contact us for help with your data or account.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data in transit and at rest. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Protect your device, backups, passwords, and access codes.

## 11. Children's privacy

The app is designed for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly ask children for data.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features or service operations change. The latest version will be available in the app or at the official policy link.

## 13. Contact

For questions about privacy, your account, or your data, contact us through the app or at [contact@rabeh.sy](mailto:contact@rabeh.sy).
